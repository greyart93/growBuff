## 1. What GrowBuff does

GrowBuff is one workspace with four helpers:

| Helper | What it does for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Agent** | Scores your Google Business Profile, suggests fixes, drafts Google posts, and helps reply to reviews. |
| **WhatsApp Chat Agent** | Answers customer questions from your own business facts, books appointments, and hands tricky conversations to you. |
| **WhatsApp Marketing Agent** | Builds festival, win-back and birthday campaigns for customers who opted in. |
| **Growth Assistant** | A chat assistant that can look things up and draft work across the other three. It asks before doing anything public. |

There is also **SEO & Maps**, a planning workspace that helps you choose local keywords, write website content and improve how the business appears in local search.

**The approval rule:** campaigns, Google posts and review replies wait for your approval. You will see **Approve**, **Approve & publish**, **Approve & send now**, or **Confirm** before those leave GrowBuff. Automations and the “auto-reply to good reviews” setting run on their own only after you switch them on.

## 3. First-time setup (onboarding)

### What this does
Onboarding collects the facts your agents are allowed to use: business details, opening hours, services, prices, tone, languages and common questions. GrowBuff turns these answers into the first version of your knowledge base.

> The demo workspace is already set up, so you may not see onboarding. A brand-new business sees this four-step wizard after its first login.

![Onboarding step 1](screenshots/02b-onboarding.png)

### Step 1 — Business

Fill in:

- **Business name**
- **Business type** (Dentist, Clinic, Salon, Gym, Restaurant, Home services, Other)
- **City**
- **Address** — used for directions and local search
- **Business WhatsApp number**
- **Website** (optional)
- **What should customers know about you?** — a short, plain description

Click **Continue**.

### Step 2 — Hours & services

![Onboarding step 2 — hours and services](screenshots/02c-onboarding-hours.png)

1. Set **Monday to Friday** opening and closing times.
2. Set **Saturday** hours.
3. Tick **Closed on Sunday** if that is true.
4. Under **Services & prices**, click **Add** for each service.
5. For each service, enter the **name**, **price in ₹** and **duration in minutes**.
6. Remove a service with the bin icon.
7. Click **Continue**.

The chat agent only offers appointment slots inside these hours.

### Step 3 — Brand & FAQs

![Onboarding step 3 — brand and FAQs](screenshots/02d-onboarding-brand-faqs.png)

1. Choose a **Brand tone**: Friendly & warm, Professional, Playful, or Calm & premium.
2. Choose languages: **English** and/or **हिन्दी**.
3. Add the questions customers ask every day under **FAQs**.
4. Write short, factual answers. Do not guess prices or medical advice.
5. Click **Continue**.

### Step 4 — Connect

![Onboarding step 4 — connect accounts](screenshots/02e-onboarding-connect.png)

1. Leave **Google Business Profile** switched on if you want Google posts and reviews in the workspace.
2. Leave **WhatsApp Business** switched on if you want the chat and campaign agents.
3. Click **Build my agents**.

GrowBuff creates the workspace and the first knowledge base. In sandbox mode these connections work locally; no Google or Meta approval is needed yet.

## 5. Inbox — talk to customers

### What this does
The shared inbox collects every WhatsApp conversation. The chat agent replies automatically using your knowledge base. You can take over at any time.

![Shared inbox](screenshots/04-inbox.png)

### Reading the list

- **AI ON** — the agent is handling this chat.
- **NEEDS HUMAN** — the agent paused and wants you to reply. This happens for complaints, medical questions, or anything it is unsure about.
- The small **#tag** shows the customer's interest, such as `#price-enquiry`, `#whitening` or `#returning`.
- The time on the right shows the last message.

Use the **ALL** and **NEEDS HUMAN** filters at the top.

### Open a conversation

Click a customer's name.

![Conversation view](screenshots/05-inbox-conversation.png)

At the top you can see:

- The customer's name and phone number.
- The **AI on / AI off** switch.
- A warning if the agent has handed the chat to you.

### Take over a chat

1. Turn the **AI on** switch off. It becomes **AI off**.
2. Type your reply in the message box.
3. Click the send button.
4. Your reply is sent as the business, and the AI stays paused for that chat.

### Let the AI handle it again

1. Turn the **AI on** switch back on.
2. New customer messages will be answered by the agent again.

### Test the agent without a real customer

1. Click **New test chat**.
2. Enter a name, phone number and a customer-style message. For example:  
   “Hi, how much is a teeth cleaning?”
3. Click **Send as customer**.

The agent replies immediately. Use this to check whether your knowledge base has the right facts. It does not contact a real phone.

> **When the AI is on**, the box at the bottom says “Simulate a customer message…”. Anything you type is treated as if the customer sent it, so you can test replies.  
> **When the AI is off**, the same box says “Type your reply…” and sends as you.

### The 24-hour WhatsApp window

WhatsApp allows free-form replies for 24 hours after the customer's last message. If that window has closed:

- You will see: **“24-hour window closed. Free-form replies cannot be delivered.”**
- The message box is disabled.
- Wait for the customer to message again, or send an approved template from **Campaigns**.

This is a WhatsApp rule, not a GrowBuff bug.

### Where the AI's answers come from

Under an AI reply you may see **Source: …**. That shows which knowledge document the answer came from. If an answer is wrong, fix the knowledge base, not the chat.

## 6. Google profile — posts and reviews

### What this does
The Google Agent checks your Google Business Profile, shows a score and a fix list, helps you publish Google posts, and helps you reply to reviews.

![Google profile score and fixes](screenshots/06-google-profile.png)

### The profile score

- **Profile score** is out of 100.
- **Good**, **Fair** or **Poor** tells you the overall state.
- **Fix these first** lists actions marked **HIGH**, **MEDIUM** or **LOW**.
- The three boxes show **Calls**, **Directions** and **Site clicks** for the last 14 days.
- The bar chart shows search views for the last 7 days.

### Publish a Google post

1. Open the **POSTS** tab.
2. Click **Draft with AI**.
3. Enter what the post should be about, for example “Weekend dental check-up slots”.
4. Choose a type: **Update**, **Offer**, **Event** or **Photo caption**.
5. Click **Draft for approval**.
6. Read the draft. It uses your business details and local keywords.
7. Click **Approve & publish** to publish now, or **Schedule** to queue it.

![Google posts](screenshots/06b-google-posts.png)

**Statuses:**

- **PENDING APPROVAL** — waiting for you.
- **SCHEDULED** — will publish later.
- **PUBLISHED** — already live.

Nothing publishes until you approve it.

### Reply to a review

1. Open the **REVIEWS** tab.
2. Find a review marked **NEW** or **NEEDS APPROVAL**.
3. Click **Draft reply**. The agent writes a reply in your brand tone.
4. Read it. Click **Redraft** if you want another version.
5. Click **Approve & post** to publish the reply.

![Google reviews](screenshots/06c-google-reviews.png)

### Auto-reply to good reviews

Turn on **Auto-reply to good reviews** if you want positive reviews answered automatically. **Negative reviews and 3-star reviews always wait for your approval.**

> In sandbox mode the Google connection is simulated. The score, posts and reviews are demo data. Real Google data appears only after a live Google connection is configured.

## 7. SEO & Maps

### What this does
SEO & Maps is a planning and writing workspace for local search. It helps you:

- See how complete your local presence is.
- Choose the local keywords worth writing for.
- Draft Google profile descriptions, page titles, service pages, local landing pages and FAQ blocks.
- Check the facts Google uses for local results: name, address, phone, hours, services and areas.

> **Important:** this is not live Google rank tracking. The score uses your GrowBuff profile and content. Demand, difficulty and priority are directional estimates. Confirm real search volume in Google Search Console or Google Keyword Planner.

### Overview tab

![SEO overview](screenshots/07-seo-overview.png)

1. Open **SEO & Maps** from the sidebar.
2. The **LOCAL SEO SCORE** is out of 100.
3. The small boxes show the five parts: **Profile**, **Google Maps**, **Website content**, **Reviews & trust**, **Freshness**.
4. **Maps readiness** shows how many of the 12 local checks pass.
5. Read **Fix these first** and start with the **HIGH** items.
6. Click **Refresh audit** after you change business details, publish posts or add content.

### Keywords tab

![SEO keywords](screenshots/08-seo-keywords.png)

1. Click **KEYWORDS**.
2. Click **Find opportunities** to generate local keyword ideas from your category, city and services.
3. To add your own, type a keyword, choose an intent (**Local**, **Transactional**, **Commercial**, **Informational**) and click **Track**.
4. Click **Track** / **Tracking** on a keyword to add or remove it from your focus set.

**What the numbers mean:**

- **Demand** — how often people are estimated to search. Directional, not exact.
- **Difficulty** — how hard it is to compete. Lower is easier.
- **Priority** — the suggested order to work on keywords. Higher is more urgent.
- **Covered / Partial / Missing** — whether the words already appear in your description, services, FAQs, posts or approved content. It is not a ranking position.

### Content tab

![SEO content studio](screenshots/09-seo-content.png)

1. Click **CONTENT**.
2. Under **Asset type**, choose one of:
   - **Google profile description**
   - **Page title & meta**
   - **Service page**
   - **Local landing page**
   - **FAQ block**
3. Enter the **Target keyword**.
4. Optionally add notes under **Anything we should include?** — parking, weekend slots, family plan, etc.
5. Click **Draft with AI**.
6. Review the draft.
7. Use **Copy** to paste it into your website or Google profile.
8. Use **Edit** to change the title, meta title, meta description or body, then **Save changes**.
9. Click **Approve** when the draft is ready for your team.

**Statuses:** **DRAFT** and **APPROVED**. Approving does not publish anything automatically; it tells your team the content is ready.

### Google Maps tab

![SEO Google Maps](screenshots/10-seo-google-maps.png)

Use this tab to keep the facts Google uses consistent.

1. Enter your **Primary local keyword**, for example “dentist in Indiranagar”.
2. Enter **Service areas**, separated by commas.
3. Enter **Secondary Google categories**, separated by commas.
4. Click **Save Maps focus**.
5. Check **Maps readiness**. It covers the primary category, name/address/phone, website, hours, keyword, service areas, secondary categories, services, recent posts, review volume, review replies and local FAQs.
6. Review **How you appear in local results** — a preview, not a guarantee of ranking.
7. Click **Copy NAP** to copy the exact **N**ame, **A**ddress and **P**hone for directories.
8. Click **Manage posts and reviews** to jump to the Google profile page.

Use the same business name, address and phone everywhere: Google, your website, Justdial, social profiles and print. Inconsistent details weaken local trust.

## 8. Campaigns — WhatsApp marketing

### What this does
Campaigns let you send a planned WhatsApp message to customers who opted in. Every campaign needs your approval, and live sends need a Meta-approved template.

![Campaigns list](screenshots/11-campaigns.png)

### Start from an occasion

The top row suggests campaigns:

- **Diwali / New Year package**
- **We miss you (60 days)**
- **Birthday month treat**
- **Quiet enquiry follow-up**

Click **Draft this** on an occasion. GrowBuff fills in the goal, name and audience, then opens the campaign composer.

### Create a campaign from scratch

1. Click **New campaign**.
2. Enter a **Campaign name**.
3. Choose a **Goal**:
   - Win back quiet customers
   - Festival wishes
   - Birthday offer
   - Follow up on enquiries
4. Choose an **Audience**:
   - All opted-in customers
   - Not seen in 60 days
   - Birthday this month
   - Tag
5. If you choose **Tag**, type the tag, for example `price-enquiry`.
6. Add any detail under **Any detail to include?**
7. Click **Draft campaign (needs approval)**.

The agent writes the message. Nothing sends yet.

### Review and send

![Campaign detail](screenshots/12-campaign-detail.png)

On the campaign page you see:

- **Message preview** — the WhatsApp bubble, personalised with each customer's name.
- **Audience** — how many opted-in customers will receive it.
- **Replies** — how many have replied.
- **WhatsApp template** — the template name and its Meta status.
- **Compliance checks** — opted-in only, STOP line present, approved template.
- **Approve & send now** — sends immediately after approval.
- **Schedule instead** — choose a date and time, then click **Set**.

### If the template is not approved yet

1. Click **Submit template for approval**.
2. In sandbox mode, the template is approved instantly.
3. In live mode, Meta reviews it. Wait for **APPROVED**.
4. **Approve & send now** becomes available only when the template is approved.

### What happens to replies

Any customer reply goes into the **Inbox**, and the Chat Agent picks it up. Interest can become a booking instead of a missed message.

### Before you press send

- Check the audience size. A campaign to the wrong tag can annoy people.
- Read the message aloud. Does it sound like your business?
- Confirm the offer and price are correct.
- Make sure the STOP line is there.
- Send at a reasonable hour, not late at night.

## 9. Customers

### What this does
Customers are created automatically from chats, imports and campaigns. This page shows who opted in and who opted out.

![Customers](screenshots/13-customers.png)

### What you see

- **Opted in** — can receive marketing messages.
- **Opted out** — must not receive marketing messages.
- **Total** — all customer records.
- **Tags** — click a tag to filter, click **Reset filter** to clear it.
- Each row shows name, phone, last seen, tags and opt-in status.

### Import customers from a CSV

1. Click **Import**.
2. Paste CSV rows into the box. The first line is the header:

   ```csv
   name,phone,tags,optin
   Neha Sharma,+919811122233,new,true
   ```

3. Use commas between values.
4. Mark `optin=true` only for customers who have agreed to marketing messages.
5. Click **Import with opt-in rules**.

GrowBuff reports how many were imported, skipped and opted in. A duplicate phone number updates the existing customer instead of creating a second record.

> **Consent matters.** Only rows marked `optin=true` receive marketing. A customer who replies **STOP** is recorded automatically and must not be added back by hand.

## 10. Automations

### What this does
Automations are the messages that go out without you opening the app.

![Automations](screenshots/14-automations.png)

### The five automations

| Automation | When it runs |
| --- | --- |
| **Appointment reminder** | 24 hours before every booking |
| **Review request** | The day after a completed visit |
| **Birthday offer** | During the customer's birthday month |
| **Win-back sequence** | Customers not seen in 60 days |
| **Chat follow-up** | Nudges quiet WhatsApp conversations |

### Turn one on or off

1. Open **Automations**.
2. Find the automation.
3. Click the switch on the right.
4. The change saves immediately.

The small line under each name shows when it last ran.

### Rules the automations follow

- Reminders and review requests respect today's bookings.
- Messages go only to opted-in customers.
- Business-initiated messages use approved templates.
- Jobs retry with backoff and move to a dead-letter queue after repeated failures.
- In this demo, the background worker or `/api/cron` runs the queue. In production, a scheduled worker does it.

> If an automation says **Not run yet**, it has not had a matching customer event. It does not mean the switch is broken.

## 12. Knowledge base

### What this does
The knowledge base is the **only** source of facts your agents may use. If a price, service, timing or policy is not here, the agent should hand the conversation to you rather than guess.

![Knowledge base](screenshots/16-knowledge.png)

### Add a document

1. Click **Add**.
2. Enter a **Title**, for example “Parking and directions”.
3. Write the facts under **Facts the agent may use**. Keep it short and specific.
4. Click **Save & re-index**.

The document is split into search chunks and re-indexed immediately, so the next customer message can use it.

### Edit a document

1. Click the pencil icon on the document.
2. Change the title or content.
3. Click **Save & re-index**.
4. To remove it, click the bin icon and confirm.

### Rebuild from the business profile

At the bottom, **Rebuild knowledge base** regenerates services, timings, FAQs and safety policy from your current business details.

> **Careful:** rebuilding replaces manual edits inside those generated documents. If you have added special wording, copy it somewhere safe first.

### What belongs here

- Prices and what is included.
- Opening hours and holiday closures.
- Services and durations.
- Parking, directions and landmarks.
- Cancellation and payment rules.
- Common questions and approved answers.
- Safety and medical boundaries.

### What does not belong here

- Guesses.
- Temporary offers you have not approved.
- Another customer's private information.
- Medical diagnoses or legal advice.

If the agent gives a wrong answer, correct the knowledge base and test again with **New test chat**.

## 15. Connecting a live WhatsApp number

> **Do this only when you are ready to message real customers.** Test with a Meta test number first.  
> Never paste access tokens, app secrets or customer phone numbers into a chat, email or screenshot.

### What you need from Meta

1. A **Meta Business Portfolio**.
2. A **WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)**.
3. A phone number for WhatsApp Business (a test number is fine to start).
4. A **system user permanent access token** with:
   - `whatsapp_business_messaging`
   - `whatsapp_business_management`
5. The **Meta app secret**.
6. A **webhook verify token** you make up. It can be any random string; you will paste the same value in Meta.
7. An **HTTPS address** for the app. For local testing, use a tunnel. For production, use your real domain.

### Step-by-step

1. In Meta App Dashboard, open **WhatsApp → API Setup**.
2. Copy the **Phone number ID**.
3. Copy the **WhatsApp Business Account ID**.
4. Create a system user token with the two permissions above.
5. Open **App Dashboard → Settings → Basic** and copy the **App secret**.
6. In GrowBuff, open **Settings → Live WhatsApp setup**.
7. Paste the **Phone number ID** and **WABA ID**.
8. Paste the **Permanent access token**.
9. Paste the **Meta app secret**.
10. Type a random **Webhook verify token**.
11. Click **Save live credentials**.
12. Click **Test connection**.
13. Copy the **Webhook callback URL** shown in GrowBuff.
14. In Meta, open **WhatsApp → Configuration → Webhook**.
15. Set the callback URL to `https://your-domain/api/webhooks/whatsapp`.
16. Enter the same verify token.
17. Subscribe to:
    - `messages`
    - `message_template_status_update`
18. Save and verify the webhook in Meta.
19. Create and submit every marketing template you plan to use.
20. Wait for **APPROVED** before sending a campaign.
21. Send a test message from a real phone to the business number.
22. Reply from the Inbox and confirm the message arrives on the phone.

### Local testing

Meta cannot reach `http://localhost:3000`. Use an HTTPS tunnel and put the tunnel URL into Meta's webhook settings. If the tunnel URL changes, update Meta's webhook URL at the same time. A stale webhook URL is the most common reason inbound messages stop arriving.

### About the 24-hour window

WhatsApp allows free-form replies for 24 hours after the customer's last message. Outside that window:

- Free-form replies fail.
- Use an approved template from **Campaigns** or an automation.
- The Inbox shows a warning when the window is closed.

### About templates

Marketing and automation messages need a Meta-approved template. Templates have a category, language and variables. Keep the wording generic enough to be approved, and include the opt-out line.

### About opt-in

Only customers who opted in may receive marketing. Keep proof of consent. Honour **STOP** immediately. Do not re-import someone who opted out.

### If Meta says the business account is locked

That is a Meta account restriction, not a GrowBuff error. Resolve it in Meta Business Support. GrowBuff cannot override a locked WhatsApp Business Account.

## 17. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No OTP appears | Sandbox mode shows the code on screen; live SMS is not configured in the demo | Use the demo number `9876543210`, or configure an SMS provider for production |
| “Approve & send now” is greyed out | The WhatsApp template is not approved | Click **Submit template for approval**. In sandbox it approves instantly. In live mode wait for Meta |
| Campaign will not send | No opted-in customers, or the template is rejected | Check **Customers → Opted in**, fix the template, resubmit |
| AI is not replying | AI is switched off, the plan limit is reached, or the 24-hour window is closed | Turn **AI on**, check **Plan & billing → usage**, or wait for the customer / use a template |
| AI gives a wrong price | The knowledge base is outdated | Edit the document in **Knowledge base**, **Save & re-index**, then test with **New test chat** |
| “24-hour window closed” | WhatsApp's free-form reply window expired | Wait for the customer to message again, or send an approved template |
| Live WhatsApp save fails | Wrong phone number ID, WABA ID, token, app secret or verify token | Recheck the values in Meta, then click **Test connection** |
| Inbound messages do not arrive | Webhook URL is wrong, not HTTPS, or the tunnel changed | Update the callback URL and verify token in Meta; subscribe to `messages` and `message_template_status_update` |
| “Connected accounts” says WhatsApp not connected, but live setup says configured | The connection status badge is separate from saved credentials | Click **Test connection**. If it succeeds, the number is reachable |
| Customer got a message after opting out | A manual import or campaign audience ignored consent | Check the customer's opt-out state and the campaign audience. Never re-add a STOP contact |
| Duplicate customers | The same phone was imported twice | Phone is the key. A duplicate import updates the existing record. Merge carefully in the database only if needed |
| Google or SEO data looks like demo data | The workspace is in sandbox mode | Connect live Google credentials and switch the connection to live |
| Assistant suggests something wrong | It is working from limited or outdated facts | Do not confirm. Correct the knowledge base or business profile, then ask again |
| Billing says active but no card was charged | Demo billing is sandboxed | This is expected. Production billing uses Razorpay or Stripe |

## 18. Safety and consent rules

GrowBuff is designed around approval and consent. Keep these rules:

1. **Approval first.** Read every campaign, post and review reply before approving it.
2. **Opt-in only.** Marketing goes only to customers who agreed. Honour STOP immediately.
3. **No invented facts.** If a price or policy is not in the knowledge base, the agent should hand over. Do not force it to guess.
4. **Medical and legal questions go to a human.** The chat agent is not a doctor or lawyer.
5. **Do not paste secrets into chat.** Access tokens, app secrets and customer data belong in Settings or a password manager.
6. **Check before scheduling.** A campaign scheduled for the wrong day or audience is hard to undo.
7. **Keep the knowledge base current.** The agent can only be as accurate as the facts you give it.
8. **Review the audit trail.** AI actions, handoffs and admin access are logged.
